1. Introduction & Scope

DURGAS Fine Jewellery ("we", "us", "our") respects your personal privacy and is committed to protecting the digital personal data of all visitors and customers ("you", "Data Principal"). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you browse, register, or make a purchase on our website, we may collect:

  • Identity Data: Full name, title.
  • Contact Data: Delivery address, billing address, phone number, and email address.
  • Transaction Data: Order history, items purchased, invoice records, and delivery proofs.
  • Payment Reference Data: Razorpay payment identifiers and transaction IDs. (We do not store your full credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers; payments are processed securely by PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways).
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, browser information, and session cookies.

3. Purpose of Processing & Legal Grounds

We process your personal data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your precious jewellery orders via insured logistics partners.
  • To issue GST-compliant tax invoices as required under Indian taxation laws.
  • To provide order tracking updates, dispatch notifications, and customer support.
  • To prevent fraudulent transactions, duplicate orders, and unauthorized access.
  • To comply with statutory reporting obligations under Indian law.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Payment Aggregators: Razorpay Software Private Limited for secure payment capture.
  • Insured Logistics Partners: Registered courier partners for secure, tamper-evident parcel delivery.
  • Statutory Authorities: Regulatory bodies, law enforcement agencies, or tax authorities upon lawful request.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement stringent technical and organizational security controls, including SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption for all data in transit, encrypted PostgreSQL database storage, rate limiting, and HttpOnly/SameSite session security. Your data is stored within certified cloud servers located within the territory of India.

6. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data processed by us.
  • Request correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for tax or regulatory compliance.
  • Nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of incapacitation or death.
