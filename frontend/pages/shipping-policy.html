Insured Transit & Safe Fulfilment

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2025 · Pan-India Insured Dispatch

Security Promise: Every piece of fine jewellery dispatched by DURGAS Fine Jewellery is 100% insured against loss or damage in transit until the moment it is handed to you.

1. Dispatch & Delivery Timelines

  • Order Processing: In-stock jewellery is dispatched within 2 to 4 business days following payment verification and quality certification checks.
  • Transit Duration: Standard delivery time is 3 to 7 business days depending on delivery pincode and location across India.
  • Custom / Made-to-Order Pieces: Customized rings, bespoke engravings, or specific gold size alterations require an estimated 10 to 14 business days for artisanal crafting prior to dispatch.

2. Shipping Charges

  • Complimentary Insured Shipping: We offer free transit-insured delivery on all prepaid orders above ₹5,000.
  • For orders below ₹5,000, a nominal shipping and transit insurance fee of ₹150 is applied at checkout.

3. Tamper-Evident Packaging & Delivery Protocol

To guarantee complete authenticity and security:

  • Your jewellery is enclosed in a signature luxury DURGAS presentation box, then vacuum-sealed within a specialized heavy-duty, tamper-evident security parcel.
  • Inspection on Delivery: Please do NOT accept or sign for any parcel where the outer security tape appears cut, torn, resealed, or tampered with. Take immediate photos and notify us.
  • OTP / Signature Requirement: For security reasons, high-value deliveries require the presence of an adult recipient and entry of a secure delivery OTP sent to the buyer's registered mobile number.

4. Tracking & Communication

Once your order is handed over to our logistics partner, you will receive an automated dispatch notification with an active tracking link via email and SMS. You can also view real-time delivery status under your Profile / Order History.
