Customer Protection & Fair Returns

Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2025 · Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

Transparency Guarantee: At DURGAS Fine Jewellery, your satisfaction and peace of mind are paramount. We offer a transparent, streamlined return and cancellation process for all authentic purchases.

1. Order Cancellation Policy

  • Pre-Dispatch Cancellation: You may cancel any ready-to-ship order within 24 hours of placement or before courier dispatch (whichever is earlier). A 100% refund will be credited back to your original payment method.
  • Post-Dispatch: Once a parcel has been handed over to the courier and tracking is issued, orders cannot be cancelled in transit. You may initiate a return upon delivery following the procedure below.
  • Custom Orders: Made-to-order, resized, or personalized jewellery cannot be cancelled once casting and production have commenced.

2. 7-Day Return Policy for Precious Jewellery

We provide a 7-Day Return Window from the date of confirmed delivery for eligible items, subject to the following conditions:

  • The piece must be completely unworn, undamaged, with no scratches or signs of wear.
  • The original security tamper-tag attached to the jewellery must remain completely intact and unbroken.
  • All original certificates (BIS Hallmark certificates, diamond grading cards), warranty cards, invoices, and luxury gift boxes must be returned intact.

3. Non-Returnable Items

  • Customized, engraved, or specially resized jewellery pieces.
  • Items returned without original hallmark certification cards or with broken security tags.
  • Damaged items resulting from handling, chemical contact, or unauthorized third-party repairs.

4. Return & Quality Verification Process

  1. Initiate Request: Email us at returns@durgasjewellers.com or contact our customer support team with your Order Number and photos of the piece with security tag intact.
  2. Insured Reverse Pickup: We arrange a complimentary, insured reverse pickup through our secure logistics partner. A tamper-evident seal bag will be provided.
  3. Laboratory Inspection: Once received at our Mumbai workshop, our gemologists and assay team inspect the piece to verify weight, purity, and diamond specifications against our records within 48 business hours.

5. Refund Timelines & Settlement

  • Following successful laboratory verification, 100% refund of the purchase price will be issued to your original payment method (via Razorpay).
  • Refunds reflect in your bank account, credit card, or UPI handle within 5 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank.
  • For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are credited directly to your verified bank account via NEFT/IMPS after you provide bank account details.
